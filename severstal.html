Unlike oil & gas majors, Severstal's footprint is dominated by Scope 1 direct emissions (63.4%), primarily from blast furnace steelmaking. Scope 3 is driven by purchased goods & services (20.3%). The use-of-sold-products category is not material for a steel producer.

The 1.5°C pathway for steel producers (Heavy Industries / ARC benchmark) requires approximately 50% reduction in S1+2 by 2030 vs. 2019, implying a 2030 budget of ~14.1 Mt. Severstal's own intensity-based target implies 2030 absolute emissions of ~21.8 Mt — more than 50% above the science-based level. 06 — Climate Governance
Reported Governance Structure
Severstal follows its Decarbonisation Strategy 2030, approved by the Board of Directors in 2023. In the same reporting year the company elaborated and approved the Conserve Biodiversity – Preserve Life Strategy, broadening its environmental governance framework beyond climate. Board-level oversight of the decarbonisation strategy is in place, though details of executive-level climate accountability and remuneration linkage are not publicly disclosed.
