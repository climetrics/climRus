04 — Pathway Gap Analysis
Reported S1+2 vs. 1.5°C Pathway
Shaded area = cumulative carbon debt (gap above pathway)
Reported S1+2
1.5°C Pathway
Lukoil 2030 Target

The 1.5°C pathway for oil & gas operators (IEA / OECD 2023) requires approximately 60–65% reduction in Scope 1+2 by 2030 vs. 2022 levels — implying a 2030 budget of ~17.5 Mt. Lukoil's own 2030 target of −20% vs. 2017 would leave emissions at 38.7 Mt, more than twice the science-based level. The red shaded area shows the cumulative gap between reported emissions and the 1.5°C pathway; the amber line shows the trajectory implied by Lukoil's corporate target.
